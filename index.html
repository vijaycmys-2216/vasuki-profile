      K. R. Vasuki is Senior Professor of Mathematics in the Department of Studies in Mathematics at the University of Mysore, working in special functions and number theory.

      His published record spans 1998–2026 and comprises 85 indexed works, concentrated on Ramanujan's theta-function identities, modular equations of prime and composite degree, continued fractions of the Rogers–Ramanujan and Göllnitz–Gordon type, Eisenstein series, and basic bilateral hypergeometric summation. Recurring venues include The Ramanujan Journal, the Journal of Number Theory, the International Journal of Number Theory, and the Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications.

      At a glance

        Publications per year

        Special functions and number theory — chiefly the identities recorded in Ramanujan's notebooks and the classical analysis needed to prove them.

        The work sits where q-series, modular forms, and classical analysis meet. Recurring objects are Ramanujan's theta functions and their level-N identities, modular equations of degree 7, 11, 21, 23, 25, and 35, continued fractions arising from basic bilateral hypergeometric series, Eisenstein series of small level, class invariants, and Ramanujan–Sato type series for 1/π.
